Inventory, source and packaging limits
-------------------------------------
licenses/sources.json records upstream URLs, tags, tree identities and file
SHA-256 checksums. It is a notice inventory, not a complete binary SBOM or an
archive of all corresponding third-party source. scripts/prepare_licenses.py
verifies local notices without network by default; refresh is developer-only.

An onedir build stores dynamic libraries under _internal. A onefile build embeds
them and extracts them to a temporary directory at runtime. Neither packaging
mode by itself establishes compliance. Temporary extraction is not a verified
persistent library-replacement mechanism. Publishers must provide the applicable
corresponding source, notices and permitted recombination/replacement materials.
Public application source alone is not all third-party corresponding source.
Keep this notice, docs/THIRD_PARTY.md and licenses/ directly accessible alongside
a binary release, even when copies are also embedded in the executable.
